## Title-craft cheat sheet (for writing your own)

1. **Hook : Method-subtitle.** e.g. *"Ghost in the Floodplain: Satellite Verification of…"*
2. **Name the real technique** in the subtitle — DEA, distributed-lag, graph neural network,
   spatio-temporal — not "AI" or "machine learning." A math panel wants the technique named.
3. **Stay defensible** — "efficiency," "inefficiency," "no detectable structure" — never accuse.
4. **Signal feasibility** — "…Using Open Data" tells the panel you can actually get it.
